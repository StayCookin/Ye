# Brag Plan: InRent

## What is this app?
InRent is a **student-accommodation marketplace for Botswana** — verified rentals near
campus, booked from your phone. Students browse verified listings, tour or request to book,
and the landlord responds; no WhatsApp groups, no guesswork, no scams.

## The angle
Show the actual student journey happening on a phone — Browse → Book → Confirmed — in the
app's real teal UI with real copy ("P2,500/mo", "Verified", "Request to book", "Booking
request sent!"). The film's promise: the messy way students find housing in Botswana becomes
three calm taps. Product-in-use, not marketing-about-product.

## Hook (first ~3 seconds)
Clean teal wash, InRent mark, one confident line: **"Student housing in Botswana — without
the guesswork."** Sets the category and the brand promise before the phone appears.

## Key moments (the middle) — the real user flow
- **Browse:** the Marketplace screen inside a phone — a search field + two verified property
  cards slide in one by one ("P2,500/mo", a teal **Verified** badge, "1 bed").
- **Book:** the Property detail — hero image, "P2,500 **per month**", amenity chips, a
  **Verified** landlord badge; a simulated tap lands on the teal **Request to book** button.
- **Confirmed:** the success state — a teal check scales into the primary-surface circle,
  **"Booking request sent!"**, and the details card (Listing · Move-in · Lease · Status:
  *Awaiting landlord*). The relief beat.

## Outro / punchline
The real InRent logo (house-and-person mark + wordmark) settles, with the app's own line:
**"Find your perfect student home."** Small: *Verified rentals near your campus · Botswana 🇧🇼*.

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result: open InRent → browse verified listings near campus → tap
**Request to book** on a place you like → **Booking request sent!** (landlord responds within
24h). The centerpiece scenes ARE this flow, shown as the real app running in a phone.

## Tone
- Preset: **cinematic + polished** (blend)
- Creative direction: a calm, premium consumer product film — Apple-keynote restraint meets a
  warm, friendly fintech/marketplace feel. The phone is the hero.
- Interpretation: 5 scenes, unhurried holds, smooth slides, one clean success beat. Motion is
  soft and confident, never busy. Light palette, lots of air.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.3s
Teal wash (#E0F7F4→#F8F9FA), small InRent mark, line settles: **"Student housing in Botswana
— without the guesswork."** Fast-in ~0.4s, hold ~2.4s.
Sequential/interaction: none. Audio: warm bed enters low. Transition: soft → phone rises.

### Scene 2 — Browse (Marketplace) — 4.7s
A phone rises into frame showing the Marketplace: search field "Search near your campus", then
two verified cards slide in one by one (image, title, location, "P2,500/mo", Verified badge,
"1 bed"). Caption above phone: "Browse verified rentals."
Sequential/interaction: yes — 2 cards arrive one by one (~5.8s, ~6.9s), held. Audio: soft drop
per card. Transition: content slides left → detail.

### Scene 3 — Book (Property detail) — 4.7s
Phone content becomes the Property detail: hero image band, "P2,500 per month", amenity chips
(Wi-Fi · Furnished · Water), Verified landlord badge, CTAs "Book a viewing" / "Request to
book". A cursor/tap ripple lands on **Request to book** (~8.96s). Caption: "Tour it, or book
it — in a tap."
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap on the primary button. Audio: one crisp click on
the tap. Transition: quick fade → success.

### Scene 4 — Confirmed — 4.0s
Success state: teal check scales into the primary-surface circle (~12.65s), **"Booking request
sent!"**, sub-line "The landlord will respond within 24 hours.", details card (Listing ·
Move-in · Lease · Status: Awaiting landlord). Hold. Caption: "Booking request sent."
Sequential/interaction: yes — check pops, card settles. Audio: one soft confirming hit.
Transition: soft crossfade → outro.

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.3s
Real InRent logo (mark + wordmark) settles (~17.91s), line **"Find your perfect student home."**,
caption *Verified rentals near your campus · Botswana 🇧🇼*. Music resolves and fades.
Sequential/interaction: none. Audio: final cadence, fade out. Transition: settle and hold — end.
